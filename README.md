DISCOVERLY

Discoverly is a social network designed to help you learn something new every day. Like other platforms, the main goal is entertainment, but Discoverly is a bit different. Here, in addition to scrolling through other people's posts, you also learn new things. It is the perfect place to share those interesting facts you have no one else to tell, while also feeding your curiosity.

Inspired by platforms like Pinterest and Reddit, Discoverly serves as yet another place to be entertained—and to learn a thing or two. It is built around posts that anyone can create to share whatever happens to be on their mind. The main goal of these posts is to share interesting content—like those random facts you stumble upon and have no one to tell—while simultaneously helping others by entertaining them and teaching them something new.

Discoverly isn't just another social network. People come to Discoverly for entertainment, but at the same time, they make the most of their valuable time by learning and broadening their horizons. Every post features a completely different story—and they’re usually fascinating. There’s no need to memorize them all (how boring that would be); you simply pick up interesting facts that you can share with others or just keep to yourself.
